# Rita Cashmere – Cashmere Knitwear Manufacturer in Nepal

Rita Cashmere is a Nepal-based manufacturer, exporter, and wholesale supplier of authentic Himalayan cashmere and premium wool knitwear. Established in 2013 in Kathmandu, Nepal, the company specializes in producing genuine Chyangra Pashmina garments crafted by skilled Nepalese artisans using traditional techniques combined with modern manufacturing standards.

With more than 13 years of experience in the knitwear and textile industry, Rita Cashmere has built a strong reputation as a trusted cashmere manufacturer in Nepal serving global fashion brands, retailers, boutiques, and wholesale distributors.

## Authentic Nepalese Cashmere Manufacturing

Rita Cashmere produces high-quality knitwear using authentic Himalayan Chyangra Pashmina fibers known for their exceptional softness, warmth, and durability. The company is a certified member of the Nepal Pashmina Industries Association (NPIA), ensuring that every product meets strict authenticity and quality standards for genuine Nepalese cashmere.

All products are carefully manufactured with premium yarns and undergo strict multi-stage quality control to maintain consistent international standards.
